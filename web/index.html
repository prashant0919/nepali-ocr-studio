What is the Synthetic OCR Corpus Builder?
This tool generates synthetic training images of Devanagari text lines — simulating real-world scan conditions like blur, ink bleed, rotation, speckle noise, and uneven lighting. Each image comes paired with COCO-format bounding box annotations (word-level JSON), which you can use to train or fine-tune deep learning OCR models (CRNN, TrOCR, PaddleOCR). Think of it as a data factory: you control the text, font, distortions — and it outputs labelled training pairs ready for your pipeline.
